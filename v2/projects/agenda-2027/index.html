Cassa RuraleAltoGardaRovereto

Developed during my internship at Graffiti Studio, the brief was to design a cover for Cassa Rurale AltoGarda Rovereto’s 2027 diary that could also adapt to other formats and applications. It needed to remain recognisably part of the brand while offering something worth looking at throughout the year.

The approved design connects the digits 2, 0, 2 and 7 into a single continuous stroke. A gradient drawn from the brand’s palette breaks that continuity into apparent fragments, creating a visual puzzle that invites the eye to piece the year together.

Alternative proposals

These are two of my favourite alternatives from the wider exploration.

The first places a 2 above a 7, both drawn from almost the same shape: rotating the 2 by 180 degrees produces the 7, apart from a small section replaced by the words “duemila ventisette”. The gradient lets parts of the forms almost merge into the background.

The second builds 2027 as a geometric mosaic of semicircles and triangles. The tightly packed shapes and contrasting tones make it the boldest of the covers shown here.
